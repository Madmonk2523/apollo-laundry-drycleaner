BEFORE YOU BOOK

FAQs.

How do I request a pickup?

Select everyday laundry, dry cleaning, or both, then provide your address, preferred date, and contact information. Apollo will follow up to confirm.

Can I request delivery instead?

Yes. Delivery requests are for items Apollo has already cleaned. Include an order reference if you have one; the team will confirm your order and the delivery time.

Is a request a confirmed appointment?

Not yet. Submitting a form sends a request to Apollo. The team will confirm scheduling, availability, and any service details with you directly.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the service and items involved. Ask for pricing in your request notes, and Apollo can share the relevant details before you proceed.

How do I know if my address is in the service area?

Include your full address in the request. Apollo will let you know whether pickup or delivery is available at your location.
